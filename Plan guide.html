What does this app do?

This is an app for people who travel on Chennai Metro. Instead of standing in long queues, you can do everything from your phone — book tickets, plan your journey, pay for parking, and a lot more.

🏠 Home Screen
Opening the app: The app shows a splash screen for 2 seconds. If you've logged in before, it takes you straight to the home screen. If it's your first time — or if you logged out — it asks you to log in.
Logging in: Enter your mobile number or email + password. Or choose "Login with OTP" to get a code on your phone. New here? Tap "Register" to create a free account — just takes 2 minutes with your mobile number.
Home screen: You see your name, today's date, and any metro alerts. Below that: 4 big icons (Book Ticket / Buy Pass / NCMC Recharge / Metro Map) and your last 5 trips with a quick "Rebook" button.
📋 Pick a Feature
→
💳 Pay (if needed)
→
✅ Done!
Choosing what to do: Tap one of the 4 quick icons on the home screen. For more features (parking, travel planner, complaints, etc.), tap the ☰ three-line menu at the top — it slides open a full list.
Making a payment: Most features need a payment. You can pay by UPI, card, net banking, or your in-app Digital Wallet. Payment takes about 5–10 seconds. If something goes wrong, your money is safe — it gets refunded automatically.
Task done! You'll see a confirmation screen. Your ticket (or receipt) is sent to your email too. Tap "Go Home" to go back, or "View Ticket" to see it right away.

QR Ticket Booking
Book a ticket for one journey
1
Choose your From station and To station from the search list
2
App shows: fare, travel time, number of stops, and if you need to change lines
3
Choose how many tickets (1–6) and if you need a senior/child discount
4
Pick your payment method and pay
5
Your QR code ticket appears on screen instantly and is also sent to your email
6
At the metro gate — just show the QR code and walk in!
✅
Ticket is valid the whole day until midnight. You can also find it under "My Bookings" anytime.
🎟️
One Day Pass — ₹199
Unlimited rides for one full day
1
Tap "Buy Passes" on the home screen
2
Choose "One Day Pass" — ₹199 for unlimited rides today
3
Pay and your pass activates immediately
4
Use it at any gate, any station, as many times as you want — until midnight
✅
Great if you're travelling more than 3 times in a day. Pass shows up under "My Passes".

Digital Wallet
Load money, use it for tickets
1
Go to "Buy Passes" → "Digital Wallet"
2
Add money — ₹50 minimum, ₹5000 maximum per top-up
3
Use the wallet balance to pay for tickets instead of UPI/card every time
4
App alerts you when your balance falls below ₹50
✅
Wallet money never expires. Note: you can't withdraw it back to your bank account.
💳
NCMC Card
Top-up your metro smart card or buy a new one
1
To recharge: Enter your 16-digit card number → check balance → enter amount → pay
2
Card is recharged instantly — you'll get an SMS confirmation
3
To buy a new card: Go to Sidebar → "Buy Metro NCMC Card" → enter delivery address → pay ₹50 card fee + initial load
4
Card is delivered to your address within 5 working days
✅
Your physical metro card — just tap it at the gate. Works across all metro networks in India.

Parking
Park your vehicle at metro stations
1
First time? Upload your Aadhaar PDF, vehicle photo, and enter your registration number. Wait for approval (takes up to 4 hours).
2
Check real-time parking availability at any station — car, bike, or cycle slots, and EV charging units
3
Select station, vehicle, date (single day or monthly) and pay
4
Rates: Car ₹40/day | Bike ₹20/day | Cycle free | Monthly pass holders get 1 free vehicle slot
5
At the parking gate — show your booking QR code to enter
✅
Park-and-ride made easy. Book monthly for savings. EV charging also available at select stations.

Choose From, To station, date, number of tickets (50–2000), and a reporting time
2
The reporting time is when all your passengers must gather at the source station
3
Pay for all tickets in one go. Discounts apply automatically (5% for 100+, up to 15% for 1000+)
4
One PDF with all tickets is sent to your email within 15 minutes
5
A metro staff member will be at the source and destination station to help the group

Perfect for school trips, office outings, or any group travel. Staff assistance included.

Assistance for Differently-Abled
Request a helper for your journey
1
Choose: From station, To station, date, time, and what kind of help you need (wheelchair, visual, hearing, or other)
2
Metro staff is assigned — one at your starting station to help you board, one at your destination to help you exit
3
You receive the name and mobile number of both staff members
4
Track status from "My Bookings → Assistance Requests"
✅
Request at least 2 hours in advance. Urgent requests (under 2 hours) are flagged to the station manager.

Lost and Found
Report a lost item on the metro
1
Tap "Report Lost Item" and choose item type (bag, phone, document, etc.)
2
Describe the item, select where you lost it (which station or on the train), add a photo if you have one
3
You get a unique ticket number — use it to track the status of your report
4
If the item is found, metro staff will call you on your registered mobile number
✅
Collect your item within 7 days of being informed. Bring a valid ID when you pick it up.

Raise a Complaint
Report any problem at the metro
1
Pick what it's about: broken infrastructure, cleanliness, staff behaviour, safety issue, etc.
2
Choose which station it happened at, describe the problem, and attach a photo if you have one
3
Submit — you get a complaint ID and a notification at every update
4
The complaint team must respond within 2 hours and resolve it within 48 hours (for infrastructure issues)

Important rules & what happens when things go wrong
⚠️ Payment problems
  • Payment failed? Your money is refunded within 24 hours
  • Payment went through but no ticket? App auto-fixes it in 5 minutes
  • UPI timed out? It's marked "Pending" — check back in 5 mins
📶 No internet?
  • Already booked tickets show the QR even offline
  • Metro map also works offline
  • For new bookings — you need internet
🎫 Ticket expired?
  • Tickets are valid until midnight on the day of booking
  • Expired QR codes are rejected at the gate
  • Day passes expire at midnight too
🔐 Login locked?
  • 3 wrong password attempts → account locked for 15 minutes
  • Use "Forgot Password" to reset via email or SMS
  • 5 failures in one day → admin reviews your account
♻️ Rebook shortcut
  • Your last 5 trips show on home screen
  • Tap "Rebook" to instantly re-fill the same journey
  • Save time — no need to type stations again
📋 Duplicate booking
  • Already booked same route today? App warns you before charging again
  • Same for Day Pass — won't let you buy two for the same day
